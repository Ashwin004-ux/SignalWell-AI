What data we collect

SignalWell AI collects: account information (email, full name, timezone); digital activity data from the browser extension (domain-level usage, time spent, session counts, late-night usage); self-reported sleep records (start/end time, duration, quality, interruptions); self-reported mood, stress, anxiety, wellbeing and energy scores and any notes you add; and AI-generated analysis derived from the above.

We do not collect passwords from other sites, keystrokes, private messages, form contents, banking information, personal files, or the contents of webpages you visit.

Why we collect it

This data is collected solely to generate the analytics, indicators, insights, and chatbot responses described in the product. We do not use it for advertising and do not sell it to third parties.

How data is stored

All data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL), isolated per user with Row Level Security policies that restrict every table to rows owned by the authenticated user. No user can query another user's data through the application.

How AI processing works

Structured summaries of your recent activity, sleep, and mood data (not raw database dumps) are sent to an AI model to generate summaries, indicators, and recommendations. How extension data is handled

The browser extension authenticates as you and sends activity data to our API over HTTPS. It never holds the Supabase service-role key or any credential broader than your own account session.
